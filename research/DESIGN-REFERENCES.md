# Sound-engineer portfolio references

Reviewed September 9, 2026 through each engineer's public site and browser screenshots. These are selected design references, not a ranked claim about the best engineers or websites.

- [Joe Joaquin](https://www.joejoaquin.com/): room photography occupies most of the opening view; warm material colors, quiet navigation, dedicated Listen, Discography, Studio, About, and Connect pages. Applied: the studio as the opening setting, a warm neutral palette, and separate pages with specific jobs.
- [Serban Ghenea](https://serbanghenea.com/): artwork and credited releases lead the experience; selected discography has a direct path to the full catalog. Applied: album artwork gets enough space, exact roles sit beside the work, and home offers a short selection leading to Music.
- [Craig Bauer](https://www.craigbauer.com/): restrained dark presentation, real portrait and control-room imagery, clear profession and contact path. Applied: charcoal framing, lighter-weight type, larger readable body text, and a visible contact link.

Only design principles were adapted. No reference-site photographs, logos, copy, artist credits, awards, or code were reused. Joel's photographs and verified project data remain the content source.

The second brief explicitly replaces the cobalt poster direction and asks for multiple pages. The result has Home, Music, Live Work, About, and Contact at individual static URLs.
